A personal archive,

La Likbør brings together photography, music, objects and selected pieces in one personal space. An archive for looking, listening and returning.

The Archive makes room for images and editorial notes. The Shop holds the garments, records and objects currently offered as selected pieces. Each has its own place within the same darker world.

This space will grow through photographs, listening notes and small collections, with room for the stories that give them meaning.
