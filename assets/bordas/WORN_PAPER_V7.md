## Prompt da borda final

Create a new ORIGINAL flat graphic texture asset: a very thin, worn, broken rectangular ink border for a black editorial website. Landscape canvas 1536x1024. Genuine transparent background. The border sits 6 pixels inside the outer edges, with irregular thickness mostly 1–4 pixels, occasional worn patches up to 18 pixels wide. Color is flat muted warm ivory-gray. Think weathered photocopy, worn paper edge, rubbed-off ink on an old printed page: thousands of tiny chips, stippled pores, incomplete hairline strokes, small dry abrasions, short scratches hugging the edge, uneven missing segments. Four sides and corners have DIFFERENT wear patterns and amounts: subtle top, more rubbed bottom-left, sparse broken right, varied left. The central 92% is entirely empty transparent. No mark should extend more than 4% inward from an outer edge. No long diagonal marks or interior cracks. Absolutely FLAT graphic art, no illumination, no gradients, no glow, no broad patches, no solid sheet, no torn outline, no 3D, no bevel, no metallic surface, no shadow, no text, no symbols, no branches or botanical forms. Imperfect asymmetric eroded edge, not a straight decorative rule. Output ONLY this subtle border on transparent alpha.

## Prompt inicial da textura de abrasão

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: high-resolution transparent distressed-paper abrasion texture for a dark editorial website, landscape 3:2 composition. Create an original scanned-print wear texture made ONLY of matte warm-gray and aged-ivory flecks, rubbed ink loss, dry paper fibers, tiny pinholes, irregular scuffed patches and fine hairline creases. It should feel like the worn edges of an old black book cover or photocopied paper, organic and tactile, not ornamental. Broad irregular density variation: heavily chipped small clusters close to all outer edges, then sparser small patches and tiny fibers inward, with a mostly empty transparent central 65% region. Thickness of worn edge varies asymmetrically from 2% to 9% of the canvas, with occasional tiny isolated chips further inward. Use real alpha transparency between ALL fragments; no solid paper sheet, no background fill, no drop shadows. No lettering, no border rules, no circles, no symbols, no plants, no branches, no spiderwebs, no roots, no veins, no glitter, no metallic finish, no decorative corner pieces, no watermark. Uneven asymmetrical margins, irregular gaps, subtle fine-scale tactile detail; avoid a uniform noise field or a clean rectangular outline. Intended to blend softly into black at low opacity around a web hero and be reused in a localized masked scuff behind a logo. No photography or people.

## Refinamento aplicado à textura de abrasão

Edit this texture into a FLAT TWO-DIMENSIONAL print distress overlay. Keep the landscape canvas and worn peripheral placement, but change the material to tiny eroded ink speckles and dry scuffed paper fibers. CRITICAL: completely remove every smooth gray/black gradient, background fill, shadow and glow. The center and spaces between flecks must be actual alpha transparency. The only visible pixels should be flat matte warm ivory-gray broken marks. No 3D relief, no bevel, no raised edge, no torn sheet silhouette. Break the large corner chunks into hundreds of tiny disconnected, irregular, porous flecks and dusty clusters, reducing their apparent size by 75%. The narrow band of wear follows the outer 2–7% of all four edges with naturally uneven density and gaps. Keep the central 80% truly transparent and clean. Like missing ink on a weathered black photocopied book jacket, expressed as the pale flecks only. No objects, no text, no symbols, no branches, no ornaments, no lines making a frame. Pure flat grunge-print texture on genuine transparent background.
